Why OwnandKeep

Sovereignty shouldn't require a computer science degree.

Bitcoin self-custody is one of the most important financial decisions a person can make. The tools to do it well exist. The knowledge to use them confidently is harder to find. OwnandKeep curates both.

We sell hardware kits on Amazon and eBay because that is where people shop. We write books because sovereignty without understanding is fragile. We build software because what exists is not good enough yet.

Setup, training, and ongoing custody help are handled by Sovreign, our official support partner. Amazon handles returns. OwnandKeep sells the tools.
